## THE LAW THAT MAKES FOILS
**Cast-audit law (2026-09-20): every lead needs a foil.** A foil is not a sidekick — it's the character whose temperament cancels the lead's excess, so the lead's traits read by contrast. Comedy, gravity, and pacing all come from these pairings. Parity was audited and closed 2026-09-20: every lead has one.

## THE FIVE LEADS + THEIR FOILS

**MS. FOX — The Librarian** (Church-only; the living archive itself — centuries old, orchestrator of heroes, clinical and cold; "Maeve" is her guarded name)
↔ **FOIL: MERLIN** — the shadow war is its own pair. She is cold logic; he is bound loyalty. (Merlin: unnamed ancient Vandal general who sacked Rome 455 AD, now a lich bound to Fox alone. Invisible to Gideon/Aurelius. Loxley is the only crew with partial awareness.)

**GIDEON CRANE — The Quartermaster** (State-side; ultimate sergeant major, Sam Elliott voice, calm under fire, zero patience for complaint)
↔ **FOIL: EZRA "WINDY" WHIPPLE** — Gabby Hayes archetype: wheezy, crotchety complainer whose screwups are always his own. **Function: relieves command tension — every tense moment gets one Windy beat.** Voice: wheezy baritone, wheeze at sentence end. Loves grease, distrusts noise, "seen too much to be impressed."

**LOXLEY — The Bard** (Church-side; flamboyant chronicler, secretly in love with Fox, keeps the Chronology of the Vixen in his lute case)
↔ **FOILS (double): AMOS "PENNY" GRUBB + MOTHER TALLOW.** Grubb = the anti-Bard — vocal-fry creak, deadpan, counts coins at the tavern counter; every grandiose Loxley moment gets one Grubb grunt ("that'll cost you," in seven forms). **Function: structural gravity.** Mother Tallow = the tavern's gravity well — low smoky rasp, unimpressed, knows everybody's tab and nobody's name, can shut down a room without raising her voice. **Function: authority at the opposite temperature from Fox's (warm vs cold).**

**AURELIUS — The Gardener** (Church-side; Socratic mentor, answers with questions, "Stercora Cum Privilegio")
↔ **FOILS (double): NETTLE + SILAS THE TINKER.** Nettle, ~15, is the anti-Aurelius: answers every Socratic question instantly, confidently, WRONG — mid-sentence realization is the comedy beat. **Function: proof the Socratic method works (argues, loses, grows) — the .dparqd thesis as character.** Silas the Tinker: gentle gravel voice, collects broken things and tries to fix them (even people, but never directly — broken thing + bench + busy hands = craft therapy). **Function: the valley's quiet web — connects tavern to world through mended objects.**

**PIKE — Dockhand/Courier** — not a foil; the generic-NPC template and the crew's logistics interface with the outside world. Voice: panic crack.
